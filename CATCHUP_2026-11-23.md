## 3. Upscaling — what's up with it

Full runbook: `TOPAZ_4K_UPSCALE.md`. Status: **proven at test scale only,
never run on a full master.**

- Method: extract the free Topaz Video MSI (`msiexec /a`, no install),
  use its bundled `ffmpeg.exe` + `tvai_up` Proteus v4 (`prob-4`), minimal
  model dir (`topaz-models-min/`: `prob-4.json` + `tvai.tz`), cut the master
  into 10 s keyframe-exact segments (`cut_for_upscale.py`), upscale each
  (`upscale_topaz.py`, resumable, frame-verified), reassemble frame-gated
  (`reassemble_upscaled.py`, hard-fails unless total = 646,461).
- Proofs on this laptop (Intel HD 520, CPU): 320x180→720p 1 s and
  **720p→3840x2160 1 s, 60 frames, exit 0** (`topaz_real1s_B2.mp4` etc.).
  Pilot segments exist in `upscale_pilot/` (6 × 10 s, manifest tracked).
- Why it stalled: **~1.2–1.5 min/frame at 4K on the laptop → >1 year** for
  646,461 frames. It is a GPU-machine job (NVIDIA + `hevc_nvenc`); the
  runbook's §10 checklist is the handoff. Nothing in the v9 work below
  changes that — v9 is 720p60 like v8.
- Newer test on this machine's GTX 1060 (in `REBUILD_V8_STATUS.md` on
  origin/main, not yet in this checkout at review time): 1 s 4K60 test
  passed at **~5.7 s/frame → ~42 days** for the full master, and the
  output **does carry the "Topaz Labs" watermark** (no `auth.tpz`).
  So: technically working, practically still not a run-to-completion
  job on this hardware, and watermarked until a Topaz sign-in.
- **Open-source resignation (Antigravity session `5d99ed74`, 2026-11-14):**
  after the watermark/cross-circumvention ask was refused, the free
  ranking given was RealBasicVSR (closest to Proteus, very heavy) >
  Real-ESRGAN / 4x-UltraSharp per-frame (sharper stills, flickers) >
  Real-CUGAN ncnn (fastest, best on AI-looking source). The user approved
  the A/B: same 1 s at 3840x2160/60f — Topaz 341 s watermarked vs
  **Real-ESRGAN x4plus ncnn-Vulkan 888 s (14.8 s/frame), no watermark**
  (`comfy-pipeline/output/ab_realesrgan_1s_4k.mp4` in the C checkout;
  stable flags `-j 1:1:1 -t 256` — multithreading lost the Vulkan
  device and produced black frames). Extrapolated: ~42 d Topaz vs
  ~110 d ESRGAN for the full master at 4K, so the settled advice was
  **1080p or selected chunks only, or a stronger GPU** — no full-master
  upscale was ever run with either engine. ESRGAN was then adopted as
  the upscaler for the separate `comfy-pipeline/gen_v9/` experimental
  AI-gen build in the C checkout (835 unique scenes cataloged, 193
  generated by 2026-11-17; installed at
  `comfy-pipeline/tools/realesrgan/realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan.exe`, plus
  the ComfyUI 4x-UltraSharp A/B driver `scripts/upscale_ab.py`).
  Note: that C-checkout `gen_v9` (AI scene generation) is a different
  thing from this G-checkout `hyperporn_v9_extended.mp4` (the v8
  extension below) despite the similar names.
- Gotchas: no Topaz login (`auth.tpz`) = large "Topaz Labs" watermark
  (no ffmpeg flag removes it); the free redistributable alone doesn't run
  (no `ffmpeg.exe`/`topaz_rlm.dll`); pointing `TVAI_MODEL_DIR` at the full
  86-model dir costs minutes of scanning per call.
